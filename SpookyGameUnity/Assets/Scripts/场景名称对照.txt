⚠️  常见错误：

❌ GameConfig: Game Scene Name = "GameScene"
   实际文件: Scene_0.unity
   结果: Scene 'GameScene' couldn't be loaded
   
✅ 修复方法：
   1. 打开 GameConfig.asset
   2. 将 Game Scene Name 改为 "Scene_0"
   3. 保存
